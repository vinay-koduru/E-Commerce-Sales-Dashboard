# E-commerce Sales Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **E-commerce Sales Dashboard** designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer trends, product performance, and regional sales.

The dashboard helps businesses monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify top-performing products and categories, and understand sales trends to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance
* Track key business KPIs
* Identify top-performing products and categories
* Analyze sales trends over time
* Understand regional performance
* Identify areas with low sales or profitability
* Provide actionable business insights through an interactive dashboard

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The dashboard provides interactive analysis of:

### Sales Analysis

* Sales by month
* Sales by category
* Sales by product
* Sales trends

### Profit Analysis

* Profit by category
* Profit by product
* Profitability trends
* Loss-making products/categories

### Customer Analysis

* Customer-wise sales
* Customer contribution
* Top customers

### Regional Analysis

* Sales by region
* Profit by region
* Regional performance comparison

## 💡 Key Business Insights

Some of the important insights identified from the analysis include:

* Identified regions contributing the highest sales.
